What kind of "UX magic" resonates with you?

These are directions, not final designs. Pick the ones that feel right for your brand.

A

Scroll-driven choreography

Sections reveal with purposeful timing — elements enter at different speeds, creating depth and parallax. Content feels like it's being presented to you, not just loaded. Think Apple product pages or Linear's changelog.

Techniques: Staggered scroll-triggered entrances, parallax layers, viewport-aware animations, scroll progress indicators

B

Typographic drama

Let type do the heavy lifting. Oversized headlines, dramatic scale shifts between sections, kinetic text that draws the eye. The hierarchy itself creates rhythm. Think Pentagram or Studio Feixen.

Techniques: Variable font weight animations, scale contrast between sections, text masking effects, viewport-filling type moments

C

Spatial rhythm & white space

Vary the density. Some sections breathe with generous white space, others are tight and information-rich. The alternation itself creates a pulse. Think Dieter Rams — less but better.

Techniques: Alternating section densities, asymmetric layouts breaking the grid, intentional negative space as a design element, visual "rest" between dense sections

D

Micro-interaction polish

The big strokes are fine — it's the details that need love. Hover states that feel tactile, transitions that have spring physics, subtle cursor effects, cards that respond to interaction. Think Raycast or Vercel's dashboard.

Techniques: Spring-based hover animations, magnetic buttons, card tilt/perspective on hover, smooth state transitions, subtle gradient shifts

E

Narrative pacing

Less about individual effects, more about the story arc. The page should have an introduction, rising action, proof points, and a denouement. Each section earns the next. Think documentary structure applied to a webpage.

Techniques: Section transitions that connect (not just stack), contextual micro-copy bridges, progressive disclosure, visual motifs that thread through sections
