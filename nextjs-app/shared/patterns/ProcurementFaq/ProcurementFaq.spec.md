# ProcurementFaq

## Intent
Answer the procurement, legal and finance questions a buyer asks before signing, in the page itself, so the FAQ structured data describes visible content.

## Interaction contract
- Keyboard: question triggers in order; Enter or Space toggles
- Screen readers: section labelled by its heading; collapsed answers are inert and hidden

## Do / don't
- Do: keep answers to published facts or owner-confirmed policy
- Don't: emit FAQ structured data for questions that are not on the page
