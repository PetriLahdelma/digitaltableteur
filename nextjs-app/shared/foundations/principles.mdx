## Status

Every component carries one of four statuses in its contract. The status says how much you can rely on it, and each step up is gated.

  caption="Component statuses"
  columns={["Status", "Count", "What it means", "To get here"]}

## Tiers

The tier describes size, not importance. It decides where a component sits in the folder tree and what it may depend on: a tier only builds on the tiers above it in this table.

<GuideTable
  caption="Component tiers"
  columns={["Tier", "What it is", "Examples"]}

## Grouping

The sidebar groups components by what they are for (Actions, Forms, Feedback, Navigation and so on), never by tier. Someone looking for a loading indicator looks under Feedback, not under Atoms.
